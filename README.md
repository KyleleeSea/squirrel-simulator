# Squirrel Simulator

A Goat Simulator-style 3D sandbox where you play a squirrel, with two-player online co-op over a direct peer-to-peer connection.

Play it: open the GitHub Pages site for this repo. One player clicks **Host online game** and shares the 4-letter code; the other joins with it.

Controls: WASD run, Shift sprint, Space jump (twice for a tail flip), F or left click headbutt, E or right click tail-grab and fling, R ragdoll, run at a tree trunk to climb it.
